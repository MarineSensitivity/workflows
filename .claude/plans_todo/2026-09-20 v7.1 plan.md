## 0. What v7.1 is, in one paragraph

A **surgical patch of the public v7 release** (usa05 grid, `mdl_seq`/`value` schema, 20 Program Areas), built the
way v7 itself was built (clone the previous DuckDB, change only what the fix touches, re-execute v7's own scoring
chunks), with two method changes and nothing else:

| # | change | one-line rule | flag (control = off) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sea-turtle core habitat** | AquaMaps suitability enters the turtle merge only where P ≥ 0.5; a range cell with no suitability ≥ 0.5 is **dropped** (no floor-of-1 fill); critical habitat still overrides, so no CH cell is ever trimmed | `TURTLE_SUIT_MIN=0 TURTLE_FILL=1` |
| 2 | **Coverage floor κ = 5 %** | a component is reportable in a Program Area only if its scored cells cover ≥ 5 % of the area; below that it is *not reportable* (no row, excluded from the composite like a component with no cells, never a zero) | `COVERAGE_FLOOR=0` |

Not in v7.1: the year-integrated productivity (decision 4), the distribution-share / national score, an occurrence
mask (`rng_occ`), per-taxon or percentile thresholds, derived ranges for AquaMaps-only taxa, any change to
Formula 9.4. All of that is v10. **Every `primprod*` row of v7b must be identical to v7's.**

2. **Suitability threshold 0.5.** AquaMaps' own default for core habitat / richness maps (aquamaps.org;
   `aquamapsdata`): an external convention, not a number tuned to our outcome, and Tim's preferred leatherback
   reading (his area-weighted p15 = 48). It leaves turtles in exactly one Alaska Program Area: Gulf of Alaska,
   9,608 leatherback cells at P 0.50–0.58, 53.5–59.4°N, 143–132°W (offshore Southeast Alaska, where Hodge &

   6:e19653, RES > 0.6 validated against surveys for marine mammals; empties Alaska), 0.4 (fails: Formula 9.4
   stretches the 0.40–0.58 remnant to 0–100, Gulf of Alaska 46.9 at 99 % coverage) and Tim's percentile rules.
3. **The floor-of-1 fill is dropped for turtles; implications understood.** It is the consequential half of

   stretches to 0–100. Accepted side effect: species lists lose turtles where they were only ever range fill

4. **The productivity fix is NOT in v7.1; it stays with v10** (`v10-2`, whose corrections block now holds the

   Michael flagged (Hope 24.1 → 27.1, Norton 16.2 → 19.9). v10's national reference (`primprod_v10` = zone mean ÷

   committed to the fix.
5. **Release `prerelease,restricted` only**, on the preview host, for Tim's review. Promotion to public
   `latest` (P5) is NOT authorized by this plan; it needs an explicit go from Ben after the review. Watch item for

Verdicts on the thread's claims:
1. *"AquaMaps leaves P ≈ 0.01–0.30 halos in sub-polar water; thresholding chops them"* (Gemini): true of the
   loggerhead only. Green and olive ridley have NO AquaMaps value in the Bering Sea (their presence there is the
   range fill); the leatherback's Gulf of Alaska values reach 0.58. **A threshold on suitability alone removes
   none of the fill**, and a percentile of the merged surface returns 1 for five of six species through p50:
   the rule as run is a no-op for them, and the Aleutian Arc still reads 21.7 at 87 % coverage.
2. *"Threshold the suitability component before range-fill and the CH override"* (Tim): adopted exactly.
3. *Step function with `W_CH` inside the thresholded branch* (Gemini): would zero CH cells below the cut. v7's CH
   override is kept instead (§3.4).
4. *"τ = 0.40, recommended by AquaMaps documentation"*: not found; 0.4 was the Ocean Health Index's old choice
   (now > 0; Halpern 2017). On v7 it fails under Formula 9.4 (table). *"75th percentile following Gasbarro et
   al."*: could not be verified; the percentile thresholds established in SDM practice are percentiles of
   suitability **at occurrence records** (P10 training presence; Liu et al. 2005, 2013; Pearson et al. 2007), not
   of the map's own values. A percentile of the map removes a fixed share of every species' area whether or not
   it has a fringe (Kemp's ridley, a Gulf endemic, would lose 15–20 % of its US area by construction), and gives
   thresholds with no common meaning (p20 = 9 loggerhead, 80 leatherback, 49 Kemp's).
5. *Lorenz / CRFD inflection*: algebraically the area-weighted mean (87 for the leatherback; his identity check
   confirms it): a hotspot criterion (Bartolino et al. 2011), not a presence criterion.
6. *Area weighting, no reprojection before statistics*: both correct, and both carried into the notebook's
   sensitivity table.
7. *"Any threshold is a re-reading of the same surface that is already wrong about Alaska … a consistency check,
   not independent evidence"* (Tim): agreed. It is why v7.1 uses an external convention (0.5) rather than a tuned
   number, and why v10 moves to evidence (`rng_occ`) and the share denominator.
8. *"I don't think that solves our Alaska turtle problem"* (Ben, 09-17): a threshold alone does not (fill +
   stretch); threshold **and** fill removal does within the ecoregional frame. *NMFS Alaska Biological Opinions
   treat turtles as discountable*: plausible, no citation given ("PDF" placeholders), not verified; cite Hodge &
   Wing 2000 (34 records 1960–1998: 19 leatherback, 9 green, 2 olive ridley, 2 loggerhead, 2 unidentified; coastal
   Southeast and Gulf of Alaska) and the OBIS zero instead.

### P1 · turtle rule in msens (Opus; worktree)
`msens::turtle_sql(turtle_ds, suit_ds, ch_keys, src, suit_min = 0, fill = 1, half_even = FALSE, ch_outer = FALSE)`:
- `suit_min`: suitability below it is absent BEFORE the multiply. `fill`: suitability assumed for an ER cell with
  none (or none ≥ `suit_min`); `NA` drops the cell unless CH covers it. `half_even = TRUE` → `round_even()` (R's
  rounding, what built v1–v7). `ch_outer = TRUE` → CH cells outside the ER footprint are kept (v7's union).
  **Defaults reproduce today's output byte-for-byte** (v8/v9 callers unchanged; existing assertions untouched).
  Roxygen states that `suit_min > 0` with `fill = 1` re-creates the fringe as a field of 1s (the 74.5 case).
- **The trap:** today's SQL hangs CH off `mult` with a LEFT JOIN (`merge.R:178-183`). With `fill = NA` a CH cell
  that has no retained suitability has NO `mult` row, so that join would silently trim it: exactly Ben's
  concern. Join CH to the **ER footprint** (`er LEFT JOIN suit`, `mult` value nullable, keep rows where
  `coalesce(mult, ch) IS NOT NULL`); `ch_outer` only decides whether CH cells outside `er` are unioned in.
  Cells 3 and 7 of the fixture below fail on the naive join.
- Fixtures in `test-merge.R` (exact outputs): ER {1:100, 2:100, 3:100, 4:50, 5:50, 7:100}, suit {1:80, 2:49, 4:51,
  5:53, 7:10}, CH {3:100, 6:100, 7:50} → at `suit_min = 50, fill = NA, half_even = TRUE, ch_outer = TRUE`:
  1 → 80; 2 → dropped; 3 → 100 (CH, no suitability); 4 → 26 (25.5 half-even); 5 → 26 (26.5 half-even, the
  regression: `round()` gives 27); 6 → 100 (CH outside ER); 7 → 50 (CH with sub-threshold suitability: **"critical
