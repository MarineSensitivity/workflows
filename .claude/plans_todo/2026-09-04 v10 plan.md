## What v10 is

Three scientific changes on the v9 merged models and grid (`global05`), released as `v10`
(`prerelease`, `restricted`, served at `preview.marinesensitivity.org/v10/`), with the v9 ecoregional
score retained for within-region use:

| # | change | why | subplan |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Distribution share, the v10 score family**: each species' risk-weighted share of its GLOBAL range, summed per component and zone, expressed per km² against the national average concentration (100 = a fair share; a location quotient), the percent of the world's importance at stake beside it, equal component weights, no richness damping, a 5 % coverage floor. | The ecoregional min–max gives every ecoregion a 100, so Table 10-2 compared curves, not turtles (Aleutian Arc 76 above every Gulf area). Defined and demonstrated on v9 data on the review page. | `v10-1 share metrics (share_sql).md` |
| B | **Primary productivity integrated over the year**: Σ monthly VGPM rate × days / days in year, polar-night and ice months = 0, instead of the mean of observed months. | Michael Rasser's v1 review (C76): Arctic areas inflated ~2× (Hope Basin 875 vs ~470 t C km⁻² yr⁻¹ in situ). The response memo committed to the fix. | `v10-2 VGPM annual integration.md` |
| C | **Ranges derived from AquaMaps** for the 15,011 scored species with no expert range, by a per-class suitability threshold calibrated (O'Hara et al. 2017 style) on the 3,466 species that have both. | Those species are scored on raw AquaMaps wherever it predicts ≥ 1; every species WITH a range is masked. Also gives them a merged global surface, which A needs as its denominator. | `v10-3 AquaMaps-derived ranges (O'Hara threshold).md` |

Plus P0 bootstrap/housekeeping (`v10-0 bootstrap + housekeeping.md`) and P5 docs/apps/release
(`v10-4 docs, apps, release.md`).

## Ground truth to build on (v9, verified 2026-09-04)

- Scored taxa 17,781 (`is_valid_usa`); with an expert range 2,770; am-only 15,011 (invertebrates
  9,224, fishes 5,165, corals 331, primary producers 282, mammals 6). Global surface 17,131 taxa
  (am-only taxa are omitted from it: `merge_models.qmd:290-292`). US scoring surface 784 M rows,
  global 3.1 B.
- Range vs suitability is by exclusion: `ds_key NOT IN (suit_ds)` is a range (`msens/R/merge.R:48-55`);
  `has_range` from the crosswalk (`merge_models.qmd:237-242`); the am-only branch is the second
  SELECT of `merge_sql()$us` (`merge.R:94-103`). Tests: `msens/tests/testthat/test-merge.R` (11 blocks,
  fixtures per rule). msens 0.41.0, 304 tests, `grid_for_ver("v10")` errors until `.GRID_VER` gets v10.
- Scoring: `score_cell_metrics.qmd:96-105` (category sums with `er_sql` by `er_mode`), `:115-137`
  (ecoregional min–max, coverage-blended), `:157-163` (composite = AVG over present components);
  `score_zone_metrics.qmd:76-91` (zone mean weighted by `pct_covered`, NOT area; the docs' `@eq-zone-agg`
  says area), `:103-108` (zone composite). The `metric` table is dropped and rebuilt every run
  (`score_cell_metrics.qmd:54-57`). `manifest_build()` lists CELL metrics only (`msens/R/version.R:397-403`).
- Productivity: `ingest_productivity.qmd` (not in the DAG; Google Drive paths) makes the two-band
  `…_2014-2023.avg.sd.tif` by `mean(na.rm = TRUE)` twice (`:147-150`, `:163-176`); `score_cell_metrics.qmd:140-148`
  reads it. Michael's comment and the committed response: `final-report_2025_review-responses_v5-MKR.md:483-528`.
- Per-species global value·area totals exist nowhere (`taxon.n_global` is a count; `taxon.range_km2` is
  the US surface). `zone_taxon.suit_er_area` (`msens/R/calc.R:491-503`) is the per-zone numerator; the
  USA pseudo-zone (`score_zones.qmd:199-210`) is the national total. The review page computes the
  global totals in `scripts/v10_review/02_global_totals.R` (30 s) and `03_am_totals.R` (2 min).
- Fallback fractions for the 27 national-only taxa: `data/us_share_fallback.csv` (committed, read by
  nothing yet).
- Version mechanics: `bootstrap_version.qmd` clones v9's `dist/` copy-on-write and verifies hashes;
  `libs/paths.R:4-5` are literals; `data/versions.csv` rows are `ver,status,access,released,title`;
  restricted serving needs `PREVIEW_REVIEWERS_V10`, `PREVIEW_RESTRICTED_VERSIONS=v8|v9|v10` and a
  Cloudflare Access app (`server/cloudflare/access.sh`); no Caddy, docs-CI or app-fork change.
- Apps: one app, every version; the flower plot hard-codes `_ecoregion_rescaled` in three places
  (`apps/scores/app.R:2447, 2526, 1010`); the choropleth legend is relative to the drawn zones
  (`:2290`); `score_v10_equalweights` would match the scored-unit discovery `LIKE 'score_%'` on its own.
- Literature settled on the page: O'Hara 2017 (alignment 63 %; thresholds matched to purpose; P > 0 ≈
  extent of occurrence); OHI moved AquaMaps from 0.4 to > 0 (Halpern 2017); Isserman 1977 (location
  quotient); Halpern 2012 / Samhouri 2012 (reference points); OECD/JRC 2008 (normalisation, weights);
  McRae 2017 (LPI weighting); Williams 1996 / Crisp 2001 (rarity-weighted richness).

## Phases, dependencies, control runs

```
P0 bootstrap ──┬── P1 productivity (VGPM_INTEGRATED)        ── independent of the merge
               ├── P2 derived ranges (AM_RANGE)  ── changes the merge → everything downstream
               └── P3 share metrics (SHARE_METRICS) ── develops + is ACCEPTED on v9; runs on v10 after P2
P4 the v10 pipeline run, once, with all three on; three control runs, one flag off each
P5 docs, apps, restricted release, close-out
```

- P1, P2, P3 are developed in parallel worktrees on the v9 data (each subplan says how to pin `ver`
  for its acceptance test). None of them publishes anything.
- P4 is a single full run of the targets pipeline for v10: `bootstrap_version → build_cell_grid →
  ingest_*` (resumed) `→ ingest_am-derived-ranges → merge_models_prep → merge_models → merge_taxon →
  score_zones → score_cell_metrics → score_zone_metrics → build_registry → publish_native
  (PUBLISH_MERGED_COG=1) → publish_score_cogs → release_marine-atlas (RELEASE_NO_S3=1) →
  build_version_manifest`, followed by the three control runs of the score notebooks (P1 off), the
  merge + scores (P2 off) and the scores (P3 off). The attribution table (per Program Area: composite
  and each component, v9 → v10, with each change's separate Δ, and `pra_score_delta` per control) is
  the deliverable of P4 and goes into `compare_versions_v9_vs_v10.html` and the release notes.
- Gates that stop the line: any v9 metric row changing under `SHARE_METRICS=1` (A must be additive);
  the merge hash under `AM_RANGE=0` not equal to the P0 baseline; `VGPM_INTEGRATED=0` not reproducing
  v9's `primprod` zone values; any species losing its US presence to an empty derived range; the
  Arctic collapsing in the mammal or bird share columns; a red msens test.
- Budget (laptop, measured on v9): merge ~3 h + merged-COG repaint ~1 h when P2 changes the merge
  (`REDO_MERGED_COG`), scoring ~30 min, share totals minutes, calibration minutes, VGPM ingest ~20 min
  from the cached tarballs. Server renders go through `scripts/srv_render.sh` only.

## How to execute with agents (sufficient, not excessive)

Rules that keep the cost down and the results reproducible:

1. **One agent per subplan step, given the subplan file and the named files only.** An agent reads
   its subplan, the files it names, and `CLAUDE.md`; it does not survey the repo (the surveys are
   done; the facts are in the subplans). If a named file:line has moved, it re-locates it and says so.
2. **Model by task**: Sonnet for boilerplate, edits with a template, running notebooks, docs text
   (P0, P1 implementation, P2 ingest/registry/docs, P3 registration/compare notebook, P5 docs +
   release); Opus for rules that must be exactly right and for reviews (P2 calibration design and
   merge fixture, P3 `share_sql()` + fixtures + notebook SQL, P5 app mode switch, every "Review
   checklist"). Fable/Mythos-class only for the final cross-phase review in P5 if the Opus review
   disagrees with a subplan. Haiku for nothing here: the mechanical work still needs R and DuckDB
   judgment.
3. **Long renders run in the background** (`run_in_background`, logs under `_output/logs/`), and the
   orchestrator checks the manifest hash or the `stopifnot` chunk, never the log prose.
4. **Reports are short**: an agent returns what changed (files), the gate results verbatim (numbers,
   hashes, test counts), and what it could not do. No narration.
5. **Worktrees for P1–P3** (`isolation: worktree`), merged to `main` in the order P1, P2, P3 after
   their tests pass; P0 and P4 on `main`. Commit at the end of each subplan with the gate numbers in the
   message body (the repo's convention).
